# Third-party licences

Monitor Buddy is a fork of [Monitor-Buddy by IdefixRC](https://github.com/IdefixRC/Monitor-Buddy) and is built with the work of the people below. This file holds the licence texts that travel with the firmware files of a release (`*-factory.bin`, `*-app.bin`, also served by the installer site), the live preview in the app (`tools/app/sim`) and the `.deb` package. Who made what, and what it is used for, is in [docs/blog/CREDITS.md](docs/blog/CREDITS.md); the short version is in [NOTICE](NOTICE).

Unity (MIT, ThrowTheSwitch) is used for the unit tests only and is not part of any released file.
